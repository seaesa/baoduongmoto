# e-moto.vn — research notes

Source: https://e-moto.vn/ (homepage). Stack on the original: Bootstrap 5.1.3, jQuery,
bootstrap-datepicker 1.9 (vi), Leaflet 1.9.4 + easy-button, Owl Carousel 2, Fancybox v4,
animate.css, Font Awesome 5 Free, Roboto 400 only (bold is synthesized).

The clone reimplements every one of these in handwritten CSS/JS. Only Leaflet, Font Awesome
and Google Fonts are loaded from CDNs.

## Behaviors reproduced
- Navbar: fixed, rgba(33,37,41,.75). Below 992px: toggler and offcanvas (400px, from the right, .3s, .5 backdrop).
- Modal #ModalReg opens automatically 10s after load; closes on the X, backdrop click or Esc.
- Bootstrap needs-validation styling (invalid/valid icons and messages).
- Datepicker only on the hero form inputs. The modal's date inputs are plain text, as on the
  original, because of the duplicate-ID quirk there. Format dd-mm-yyyy, startDate today,
  opens above the input when it fits, no autoclose; picking a From date clears To and limits it.
- Owl: loop, autoplay 3s, 250ms, drag/swipe. Arrows and dots appear only when there are more
  items than fit per view (below 1000px).
- Lightbox: hash #hinhanh-nhamau-N, counter, zoom, slideshow, fullscreen, thumbnail strip, arrows, Esc.
- Map: 6 stores, labels, popups with "Chỉ đường", a locate button, and a nearest-store badge.
- Product card hover (shadow, white, image brightness .7); every link turns #dc3545 on hover.
- Call button pulse, Zalo tada animation, heartBeat icon, mobile bottom-social bar (≤767).

(`content/data.json` + images come from `scripts/extract-content.py <cache-dir>`). Each page is `<path>/index.html`, so URLs have no `.html`.

Behaviour notes for inner pages:
- Booking modal on product pages: no auto-open; "from" date picker has `todayBtn` ("Hôm nay", only moves the view) + `autoclose`; picker z-index = max ancestor z-index + 10 (shows above the modal, as bootstrap-datepicker does).
- Product image slider: Bootstrap 5 slide (5s interval, pause on hover, .6s, indicator thumbnails, swipe).
- Links to pages that were not cloned (other languages, news pages 2–11, other news, tags, categories) are `#`. No reference to e-moto.vn remains.
- Forms validate like the original; a valid submit is not sent anywhere (no backend) — the form resets.
